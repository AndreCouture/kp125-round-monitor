Sensor wiring plan

How the SEN55 and SCD41 tap into the round display's own GPIOs, what the finished setup sits like on the desk, and two ways to work a sensor mount into the stand.

Wiring

One tap into the board's existing 12-pin connector. The SEN55 adapter sits in the middle of the I2C bus: it boosts power for the SEN55's fan and passes the same two wires on to the optional SCD41.

Physical layout

Schematic side view, not to exact scale. The sensor mount sits beside the existing stand; the four wires leave through the cap's notch that already exists for the 12-pin cable.
